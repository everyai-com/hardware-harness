**@alexflorias** (Alex) · 4 likes · Thu Sep 10 20:37:10 +0000 2026

Smells fishy, most every micro controller/chip vendor has minimum order quantities. Typically in the prototyping process you’ll work with a vendor that has those parts on hand to make you a prototype, or you contact individual suppliers for 1 off components at a GREATLY increased cost. Call intel, and try to get 1 single USB4/TB4 IO controller Call Texas Instruments and try to get 1 single USB4/TB4 DAC Call Parade Technologies and try to get 1 single USB-C PD controller Chinese suppliers have these parts on hand so they can make a prototype for you, it’s much harder to order parts for DIY assembly. On top of that, only recently have machine shops like @sendcutsend started providing hobbyists with low quantity CNC parts for things like chasis/knobs
